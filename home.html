About us

Who's running the war

This project started with a simple realization: the community deserved better. For years, players endured careless server owners, poor communication, and neglected feedback simply because no higher-quality alternative existed. Laurence founded this server to break that cycle. We are committed to active administration, transparent updates, and putting our playerbase at the core of every decision we make.

This server started 5 years ago, it aims to help players who are looking for a millitarian warfare based server, with factions and teams we aim to accomodate every type of player, what makes us different is our unrelenting determination to see this server thrive.

Our moderation approach is quite lenient compared to other servers. You have minimum of 2 offenses until you are banned depending on the offence. Our long term vision for this server is to see it grow and take its place within the minecraft community.
