General conduct

Community rules

01

Respect all players

No harassment, hate speech, or targeted bullying — in-game or in Discord.

02

No cheating or exploits

X-ray, hacked clients, dupes, and known exploits are bannable.

03

No griefing outside war rules

Combat and base raiding is allowed only under faction war rules

04

Keep chat appropriate

No NSFW OR suggestive content, spam, or advertising other servers.

05

Follow staff instructions

Staff decisions are final in disputes; appeals go through the Discord ticket system.
